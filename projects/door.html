SUMMER RESEARCH INTERNSHIP · JUN 2024 – AUG 2024

Perception and Navigation System for Robotic Door Opening

Humanoid Robotics Lab · KIST

SLAMObject Detection6D Pose Estimation

LiDAR-inertial mapping and vision-based door-handle localization for a mobile manipulation task.
LiDAR / camera→3D SLAM→Handle detection→6D pose estimation

Overview

As a summer research intern at the KIST Humanoid Robotics Lab, I developed mapping, navigation and camera-perception components for a door-opening task targeting a wheeled humanoid robot. The system needed to represent the surrounding indoor space while also identifying the relatively small door handle and estimating its spatial pose.

I connected a 3D LiDAR-based mapping workflow to a 2D navigation representation, then developed a custom door-handle detection dataset from videos I recorded myself, trained and refined YOLOv7, and integrated FoundationPose for 6D pose estimation. Simulation was used to evaluate the SLAM and navigation components; the camera-perception pipeline was developed using the collected image dataset. These components formed parts of the larger door-opening pipeline, rather than a claim that the entire manipulation task was autonomously completed.

3D SLAM with PCD mapping, 2D occupancy-grid navigation, and 6D handle pose estimation.

Method & Implementation

  • 3D Mapping and 2D NavigationBuilt Fast-LIO2-based 3D PCD maps and converted them to 2D occupancy grids for navigation. Switched to HDL localization after Fast-LIO2 relocalization diverged near narrow corners; simulation evaluated SLAM and navigation only.
  • Custom Door-Handle DatasetRecorded door-handle videos and extracted frames; manually labeled the first several dozen samples, auto-labeled approximately 1,100 in Roboflow, reviewed annotations, and augmented the dataset to 12,063 annotated images.
  • YOLOv7 Training and RefinementTrained YOLOv7 on the custom dataset and reduced false positives by introducing background images; the gallery presents the final model's Precision–Recall graph.
  • 6D Pose EstimationPrepared model assets for the three door-handle types in the target indoor environment using CAD models provided by other team members. In Blender, I extracted point clouds, downsampled them to a suitable density, and added UV mapping for reflective surface appearance. Using the prepared assets, I verified real-time FoundationPose inference from live RGB and depth camera streams without additional fine-tuning.

Validation & Context

The internship involved 3D LiDAR mapping, conversion to an occupancy-grid navigation map, and a change from Fast-LIO2-based relocalization to HDL-based localization after instability in narrow corners. Gazebo simulation was used for SLAM and navigation evaluation, not for the door-handle perception experiments. I created a custom dataset from recorded videos, used Roboflow-assisted annotation and augmentation to obtain 12,063 annotated images, trained YOLOv7, and addressed false positives with background data. The gallery includes the final model's Precision–Recall graph and a FoundationPose-based 6D pose-estimation demonstration.

The public page describes these individual subsystem contributions, not a verified end-to-end door-opening success rate.
